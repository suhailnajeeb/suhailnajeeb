# Hi, I’m Suhail 👋

I’m a computer vision researcher and engineer, and a PhD candidate at the University of Melbourne. My research explores how detection-transformer models can become more robust, explainable, and effective in real-world settings—especially when objects are small, difficult to localise, or captured under changing conditions.

Alongside my research, I build and test perception systems for robotics, with experience spanning object detection, stereo depth, 3D localisation, and edge deployment. I’ve also contributed to university–industry collaboration, software delivery, and teaching.

I’m interested in the space where rigorous research meets practical engineering: building computer vision systems that are reliable, understandable, and useful beyond the lab.

## Selected public work

- [Spatial Feature Fusion](https://github.com/suhailnajeeb/spatial-feature-fusion) — 3D lung-tumour segmentation using spatial feature fusion
- [Traffic-sign Detection](https://github.com/suhailnajeeb/traffic-sign-detection-vip2017) — Detection, tracking, and classification of traffic signs
- [Lung Segmentation — IEEE VIP Cup 2018](https://github.com/suhailnajeeb/lungseg-vip2018) — Lung segmentation from 3D CT scans
